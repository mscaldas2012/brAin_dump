# English Omit Needless Words

Use this skill when prose is wordy — sentences carrying words that do not add meaning, clarity, or necessary rhythm. The principle is economy: not that sentences should be short, but that they should contain no unnecessary words.

Source concept: "Vigorous writing is direct. A sentence should contain no unnecessary words, a paragraph no unnecessary sentences, for the same reason that a drawing should have no unnecessary lines and a machine no unnecessary parts. This requires not that the writer make all sentences short, or avoid all detail... but that every word tell." (Strunk, *The Elements of Style*, 1918)

## Rules

- **Hollow openers**: sentences that begin with *"It should be noted that," "It is interesting to observe that," "It will be seen that," "The fact is that," "The point is that"* are delay constructions — cut the opener and start with the substance. *"It should be noted that the window was open"* → *"The window was open."*
- **"The fact that" constructions**: replace with a direct clause or restructure. *"Owing to the fact that he arrived late"* → *"Because he arrived late."* *"Despite the fact that she objected"* → *"Although she objected."* *"In spite of the fact that"* → *"Although."* *"The fact that he had refused"* → *"His refusal"* or *"that he had refused."*
- **Redundant modifiers**: when the adjective or adverb repeats information already in the noun or verb, cut it. *"Blue in color"* → *"blue."* *"Nodded her head"* → *"nodded."* *"Advance planning"* → *"planning."* *"Past history"* → *"history."* *"Completely finished"* → *"finished."*
- **Redundant pairs**: paired synonyms joined by "and" or "or" where one word does the work of both. *"Each and every"* → *"every."* *"True and accurate"* → *"accurate."* *"First and foremost"* → *"first."* *"Null and void"* → *"void."* Cut to the stronger word.
- **Wordy prepositions and connectives**: replace bloated prepositional phrases with single words. *"In the event that"* → *"if."* *"At the present time"* → *"now."* *"In the near future"* → *"soon."* *"On the occasion of"* → *"when."* *"For the purpose of"* → *"to."* *"In order to"* → *"to."*
- **Unnecessary relative pronouns**: *"the man who was sitting beside me"* → *"the man sitting beside me."* Cut *"who was"* and *"which was"* wherever the participial or adjectival form serves.

## Objective Rubric

- No sentence in the revision opens with a hollow delay construction (*it should be noted that, it is interesting to observe that*).
- No *"the fact that"* or *"owing to the fact that"* construction remains where a direct clause or single word serves.
- No modifier in the revision repeats information already carried by the word it modifies.
- No redundant pair remains (*each and every, null and void, true and accurate*) where one word carries the full meaning.

Pass only when every applicable check passes.
